CampusVote Features

Official CHARUSAT Election Platform Capabilities

Host Your Own Election

Every student and faculty member can become a Host/Admin to create custom election systems for councils, clubs, and societies.

Dual Voting Access

Cast your vote seamlessly using official CHARUSAT Google Single-Sign-On or enter a secure 6-digit election code.

100% Anonymous & Tamper-Proof

Zero link stored between voter identity and selected ballots. Cryptographic receipt hashes ensure voter privacy & secrecy.

Targeted Eligibility Scoping

Restrict voting access to specific CHARUSAT Institutes (CSPIT, DEPSTAR, CMPICA, etc.), Departments, or Academic Batches.

Real-Time Turnout & Live Results

Monitor live voter turnout, view candidate leaderboards, and audit public voting logs instantly upon poll completion.

Step-Up Security & NOTA Option

Step-up authentication for host administrative actions, single & multi-choice ballots, and NOTA (None Of The Above) support.
